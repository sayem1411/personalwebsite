Intro

Graphite recovery from lithium-ion battery black mass, mineral processing, and critical materials extraction.

I am a Metallurgical Engineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Utah with more than six years of combined research and steelmaking experience. My work focuses on separating graphite from cathode active materials in lithium-ion battery black mass, and on recovering critical materials from complex feedstocks more broadly.

My current research uses mechanical pretreatment, binder removal, particle size control, and froth flotation. I have reached a 1.70 graphite enrichment ratio at room temperature with low reagent use, and a final graphite product of 96.20% grade in my pretreatment work. I design and analyse those experiments with Design-Expert, response surface methods, and machine learning.

Before joining Utah, I completed a B.S. in Materials and Metallurgical Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), and worked for three and a half years as a shift manager in a steel melting shop running a Quantum Electric Arc Furnace and Ladle Furnace.

Research Experience

Aug 2023 - Present
University of Utah - Graduate Researcher

  • Separate graphite from cathode active materials in Li-ion battery black mass using mechano-chemical pretreatment, PVDF binder removal, and froth flotation.
  • Optimised collector, frother, flocculant, and dispersant dosages, solids loading, and two-stage flotation. Best combined work reached 89.70% graphite recovery at 63.64% grade, with an enrichment ratio of 1.68 to 1.70.
  • Run designed experiments in Design-Expert, including Plackett-Burman screening and response surface designs, and compare linear, quadratic, neural network, and random forest models for grade and recovery.
  • Recover gallium from mine waste by reverse flotation, with over 70% Ga reporting to concentrate. Also work on copper recovery by counter-current leaching, rare earth recovery from bastnasite ore, and copper solvent extraction.
  • Characterise feed, concentrate, and tailings with ICP-OES, SEM/EDX, XRD, FTIR, TGA/DSC, Raman spectroscopy, and particle size analysis.
  • Teaching Assistant, Mineral Processing I, Spring 2026.
